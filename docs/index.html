teams-unfloat

Minimizes Microsoft Teams' floating meeting window on macOS.

Leave the meeting for another Space or app, and the floating copy of the call goes to the Dock instead of covering your work.

Install Source on GitHub

What Teams gets wrong

The meeting follows you everywhere. In a Teams call, switch to another Space or put another app in front, and Teams opens a small floating copy of the meeting over everything, on every Space. It sits on top of the window you went there to use, and Teams has no setting to turn it off.

What teams-unfloat does

Minimizes the floating window

The moment it appears, it goes to the Dock, without the minimize animation. Your call goes on, and the meeting window stays where you left it.

Gives it back when you ask

Click it in the Dock and it stays, until you go back to the meeting window or minimize it yourself.

Touches nothing else

Only that one window. Teams is never quit, and the meeting window is left alone.

Small on purpose

  • One open-source binary, run as a background service. No app, Dock icon or menu bar.
  • One permission, Accessibility. No Screen Recording, no network.
  • About 8 MB of memory, and no timers: it wakes only when something happens.
  • Controlled with a few commands.

Install

macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. In Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/np25071984/teams-unfloat/main/install.sh | sh

Or with Homebrew:

brew tap np25071984/teams-unfloat https://github.com/np25071984/teams-unfloat
brew trust np25071984/teams-unfloat
brew install teams-unfloat
brew services start teams-unfloat

On first start, teams-unfloat asks for Accessibility permission and opens the right pane of System Settings: turn on teams-unfloat there. Everything else, from upgrading to uninstalling, is in the README.
